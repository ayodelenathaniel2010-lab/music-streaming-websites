Top 15 Artists

About us

We help music fans see who the world is listening to right now, and jump straight into their songs.

What we do

Top 15 Artists is a simple guide to the most-listened-to artists on Spotify. Each artist gets a card with their monthly listener count and three of their most popular songs, so you can find something to play in a few seconds.

Every artist name opens a search for their playlists on Spotify, and every song title opens a search for that song, so you can start listening right away.

How the ranking works

Artists are ranked by Spotify monthly listeners, which counts the number of different people who played an artist's music in the last 28 days. It is a good measure of who is popular right now, and it is different from total streams over an artist's whole career.

The bar under each listener count compares that artist to the number one artist, so you can see the gaps at a glance.

Where the numbers come from

Monthly listener figures are Spotify's public numbers as of 11 October 2026, and they change every day. Song figures are all-time Spotify streams. Some are exact, and the rest are rounded down to the nearest billion, so "2B+" means more than 2 billion.

We are not affiliated with Spotify or any of the artists shown. Names and song titles belong to their owners.
