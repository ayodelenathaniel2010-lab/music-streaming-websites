Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

This site is simple. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and no ads. We collect very little about you, and we do not sell your information.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form, pressing Send opens your email app with your message ready to send. We only receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message. We use it to reply to you, and we keep it only as long as we need to.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may keep basic server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visited. This is used to run and protect the site. We do not use it to identify you.

Cookies

See our cookies page for details.

Other websites

Links on this site lead to other websites, including Spotify, Instagram, and Facebook. Those sites collect their own information under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask us what messages we hold from you, or ask us to delete them, by using our contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Send us a message.
