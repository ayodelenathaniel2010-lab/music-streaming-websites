Cookie policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device to remember things about your visit, such as your settings or what you looked at.

Our use of cookies

At the moment this site does not set any cookies of its own. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Cookies from other sites

When you click a link to Spotify, Instagram, Facebook, or another site, that site may set its own cookies. We do not control them. Please check their cookie policies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not affect how this site works.

If this changes

If we add tools that use cookies, such as analytics or ads, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
