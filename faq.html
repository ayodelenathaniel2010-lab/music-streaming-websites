Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how the site works.

Where do the numbers come from?

Artist listener numbers are Spotify's public monthly listener figures. Song figures are all-time Spotify streams, and some are rounded down to the nearest billion, so "2B+" means more than 2 billion.

How are the artists ranked?

By Spotify monthly listeners, which counts the different people who played an artist in the last 28 days. It shows who is popular right now, not who has the most streams over a whole career.

How often are the numbers updated?

We update them by hand, so they can lag behind Spotify, which changes its numbers daily. The date on the home page tells you when the listener numbers were taken.

Can I listen to music on this site?

No. We do not host or stream any music. Artist names and song titles are links that take you to Spotify, where you can play them.

Why does clicking a song show search results?

The links open a Spotify search for that song and artist, so you can pick the version you want. The right track is usually the first result.

Are you connected to Spotify or the artists?

No. We are an independent site and are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Spotify or any artist shown.

I found a mistake. What should I do?

Please send us a message and say which artist or song is wrong. We will check it and fix it.

Can you add another artist?

You can suggest one through our contact page. The list currently shows the 15 artists with the most monthly listeners.
